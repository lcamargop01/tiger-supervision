What is a supervision agreement?

A supervision or collaborative practice agreement is the written arrangement between an advanced practice clinician — a nurse practitioner, physician assistant, or other licensed clinician — and the physician responsible for working with them. It names the physician, sets out what the clinician may do and prescribe, and specifies how the physician is reached and how charts are reviewed.

Rules vary by state and by clinician type. Some jurisdictions grant independent practice after a transition period; others require a physician throughout a clinician's career. Each page here summarizes the rules for that state and cites the statute or administrative code behind every term.
